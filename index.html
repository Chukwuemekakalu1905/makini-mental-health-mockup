Introducing Makini

Makini Mental Health Center LLC

We believe everyone deserves a chance to live a fulfilling and meaningful life. That’s why we’re dedicated to providing quality healthcare services focused on your mental and emotional needs.

We understand that seeking help for behavioral health issues and addiction can be difficult and overwhelming. You’re not alone in this journey, and we’re here to support you every step of the way.

As an outpatient behavioral treatment program in Arizona, our team of mental health professionals takes time to listen and works with you to develop a personalized treatment plan. We provide assessment, screening, counseling, and treatment for a wide range of mental health concerns.

Our Commitment

Mission, vision, and goals

Mission

Our mission is to provide personalized and holistic care to empower individuals with mental and behavioral health needs. We commit to creating a safe and inclusive environment that reflects our respect for the diversity and dignity of every person we serve.

Vision

We envision a community where all individuals have access to comprehensive mental and behavioral health resources to support their well-being in transitional living environments.

Goals

  • Heal physically, mentally, and spiritually
  • Address the root cause of behavioral health issues and/or addiction
  • Confront systemic family issues
  • Develop a treatment plan
  • Aid clients to transition with success
  • Navigate transitional facilities and coordinate care

What We Do

Our services

At Makini Mental Health Center LLC, we provide assessment, screening, counseling, and treatment to help individuals in the community achieve optimal mental wellness. Our team works with you to develop a personalized treatment plan that addresses your unique needs and goals.

Alternative Therapies

Non-medical treatment to complement traditional counseling and promote healing. Practices such as meditation, yoga, and massage therapy can help manage symptoms of anxiety, depression, and PTSD. Our team can help you integrate these practices into your current plan or explore them as a standalone treatment.

Counseling

Safe and supportive spaces for individuals, couples, and families to work through challenges. We offer individual, group, marriage, and family counseling, using evidence-based techniques to help clients manage symptoms of conditions such as anxiety, depression, and trauma.

Case Management

Personalized support to help you navigate the mental health system. Our team can help you access the resources you need, from scheduling appointments to advocating for your needs, so you don’t have to go through this alone.

Medication Management

Helping you manage medications to support mental health treatment, with personalized plans monitored for effectiveness and side effects.

Mood Disorders

Understanding and treating the fluctuations of mental health.

Movement Disorders

Navigating the complexities of neurological conditions.

Personality Disorders

Identifying and addressing patterns of behavior to achieve lasting recovery.

Psychosocial Rehabilitation

Skills-based treatment and support to help individuals with mental health challenges.

Psychotherapy

Healing the mind through evidence-based therapy.

Substance Abuse

Supporting individuals on their journey to recovery from addiction.

Telepsychiatry

Convenient and accessible mental health care through virtual visits.

Transitional Housing

Safe and supportive transitional housing for individuals in recovery.

Primary / Wound Care Services

Comprehensive medical care to support overall health and wellness.

Programs Offered

Specialized programs for your recovery

Our specialized services are designed to support you on your journey to recovery.

Behavioral Health Clinic

Comprehensive mental health services for individuals struggling with emotional and behavioral issues. Licensed professionals provide evidence-based therapies for concerns including depression, anxiety, trauma, and addiction.

Psychiatric Evaluation and Assessment

Thorough assessments and evaluations to identify underlying issues and develop accurate diagnoses and personalized treatment plans.

Outpatient Counseling

Individual, group, and family counseling to help clients gain insight, learn coping skills, and develop healthy relationships.

Medication Management

Personalized medication plans for individuals with mental health disorders, monitored and adjusted for effectiveness and minimal side effects.

Adjunct Clinical Home Services

In-home counseling and psychiatric services for individuals who are unable to come to the clinic, offering personalized and convenient care.

Life Skills Management

Education and counseling to help clients develop practical skills, healthy habits, stress management, and positive relationships.

Screening, Assessment, and Treatment Planning

Thorough assessments that lead to personalized treatment plans addressing each client’s unique needs.

DUI Treatment / Screening Education

Education and counseling for individuals charged with DUI offenses, focused on understanding the impact of behavior and developing healthier coping strategies.

Domestic Violence Treatment (Misdemeanor)

Counseling and therapy for individuals charged with misdemeanor domestic violence offenses, with support to understand impact and prevent future incidents.

Drug Testing (Collection)

Drug testing collection services for individuals who require testing for legal or employment reasons.

Substance Abuse Education

Education and counseling for individuals struggling with substance abuse, using evidence-based approaches to support recovery and prevent relapse.

Meet Our Team

The people behind our care

Our staff is the heart of our organization. Each member brings a unique set of skills and experiences, and together we work toward our mission of improving the lives of our clients.

Dr. Stanley Kidiavayi, DNP, CWS

Makini Mental Health Center LLC

With an academic background and years of experience in the field, Dr. Stanley Kidiavayi, DNP, CWS, is committed to improving the lives of his patients and helping them achieve lasting recovery.

Throughout his career, he has remained committed to the belief that everyone deserves access to high-quality mental healthcare. His compassionate approach and commitment to patient well-being have earned him the respect of patients and colleagues in the mental health community.

Dr. Kidiavayi has devoted most of his practice to wound care. He also has extensive experience in primary care, urgent care, home health operations, and as a hospitalist at Sunrise Hospital. He graduated Magna Cum Laude with a Doctor of Nursing Practice degree from Chatham University in Pittsburgh, PA. He is a Certified Wound Specialist Diplomate, which enables him to manage complex wounds using advanced technology. He prefers actions with tangible results rather than promises and words.

Ms. Ana Doble

Makini Mental Health Center LLC

Ms. Ana Doble has dedicated her expertise to the healthcare industry. Since 2006 she has worked with adults and provided therapy regardless of race, gender, or status. She is passionate about her patients’ well-being and is committed to quality care, with the goal of helping each person become independent and back on their feet.
